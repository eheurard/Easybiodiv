Security

The site is only reachable over HTTPS (HSTS enabled). Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2), forms are protected against cross-site request forgery (CSRF), and administration features are restricted to authorised accounts.

Your rights

  • the right to set instructions for what happens to your data after your death (article 85 of the French Data Protection Act).
