A cookie is a small file placed on your device by the site you visit. Local storage (localStorage) is a similar mechanism built into your browser. Both are governed by article 82 of the French Data Protection Act. This English version is provided for convenience; the French version prevails.

What Easybiodiv uses

Easybiodiv uses no advertising, analytics, social media or tracking cookies. The only cookies set are strictly necessary for the site to work or for a feature you requested. They are exempt from consent (CNIL guidelines and recommendation of 17 September 2020), which is why no pop-up asks you to accept them.

Browser local storage

This information stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. It only remembers display preferences:

KeyPurpose
themeLight or dark mode
sidebar-collapsedSide menu collapsed or expanded
selected-company-idLast company viewed, kept from one page to the next
overview-mode, esg-city-compareLast settings of the overview and comparison views
cookie-notice-dismissedStop showing the cookie information banner

Third-party services

Base maps and chart libraries are loaded from third-party services (details in the privacy policy). They do not set cookies through Easybiodiv.

Deleting or blocking this data

You can clear cookies and site data at any time from your browser settings ("Privacy" or "Browsing data" section). Blocking the sessionid and csrftoken cookies will, however, prevent you from logging in to your account.
